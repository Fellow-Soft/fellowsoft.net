A journal and a designer for what you grow.

Keep cuttings, draw a garden, and care for what you plant through the year.

Cuttings

Pictures, quotes, links and words, with tags and hearts. Every cutting can be about a real plant, bed or house plant, and can be taken onto your own page or into a plan.

The Plots

The garden’s projects by year, each with its budget and its dream. Draw the dream on the plan before anything is planted, cost it, then plant it, and the year starts asking for its care.

The Plan, the Plants and the Year

A drawn plan of the garden, a hundred plants drawn by hand, the wheel of the year, care by the month and the household’s own frost dates.
